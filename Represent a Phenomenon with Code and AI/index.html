What does this model preserve?

It preserves the relationship between a notification, the other person's gaze, their response, and the length of a pause. In the call scenario, you experience about 12 seconds of silence yourself.

It does not show who is calling, what the message says, or the person's habits. It cannot prove that calls always interrupt conversations.
