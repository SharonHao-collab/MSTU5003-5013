# Meet Miso

A small interactive sketch about how distance and tone change a cat's response. Open `index.html` in a browser. Choose one of the three microphone voice options and click the location pin inside the room. The pin toggles between approaching and stepping back; the voice can be changed at any time.

The two inputs combine into four observable states: watching from afar, rubbing against you, bouncing excitedly, and leaping away in fright. The page changes Miso's movement, face, room color, and response text. No images, sound files, account, or network connection are needed.

For the assignment reflection, record what you actually tried, what surprised you, and what you changed. Add your own GitHub and AI conversation links after publishing them.
